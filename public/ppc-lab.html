PowerPC → IR → WebAssembly

This page decodes a small, real subset of big-endian PowerPC instructions, converts them to a universal IR, emits a WebAssembly module, and executes it in Chrome. It is the first compiler slice for the future Xbox 360 backend—not a full XEX runtime yet.

Quick demo

Compiles addi r3,r0,40; addi r3,r3,2; ori r3,r3,0. Expected result: 42.

Supported right now

addi addis ori oris xori andi. andis. add subf or xor and

Branches are decoded but deliberately rejected by the current WASM emitter.
